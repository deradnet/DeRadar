**ABOUT THE TECHNOLOGY**

**AR.IO**: DeRadar leverages AR.IO gateway services to provide fast, reliable access to decentralized data. AR.IO creates a permissionless network of gateways that serve data from the Arweave blockchain.

**Arweave**: All historical flight data is permanently stored on the Arweave blockchain, ensuring data permanence, immutability, and accessibility. Arweave provides a revolutionary permanent storage solution that makes data available forever.

**Derad Network**: DeRadar is developed and maintained by the Derad Network, a community dedicated to building decentralized aviation tracking infrastructure. The Derad Network operates receivers worldwide to collect ADS-B data from aircraft.

**HOW IT WORKS**

Aircraft broadcast their position, altitude, and other data using ADS-B (Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast) technology. The Derad Network operates community receivers that capture these broadcasts. This data is then stored permanently on the Arweave blockchain and made accessible through AR.IO gateways, creating a decentralized, transparent, and permanent record of aircraft movements.
